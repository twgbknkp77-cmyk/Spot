Spot Support

Need help with Spot, want to report an issue or have feedback about the app? Contact support and include as much relevant detail as you are comfortable sharing.

Contact Support

spotfitness.support@gmail.com

When reporting an issue

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, Spot version and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not send sensitive fitness information unless it is needed to explain the issue.

Your fitness data

Spot stores fitness data locally on your device. Support cannot remotely access or recover your records. Keep exported backup files secure.

Privacy enquiries

The same email address can be used for questions about Spot’s privacy practices. You can also review the full privacy policy.
