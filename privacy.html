Spot – Fitness Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last revised: 12 November 2026

About Spot

Spot – Fitness Tracker is published by Luke Atkins, an individual developer. This policy explains how Spot handles information when you use the app.

Data stored on your device

Spot stores its app data locally on your device. This may include programmes and planned exercises; completed workouts; sets, repetitions, weights and cardio results; effort ratings; exercise notes and workout comments; bodyweight measurements and goals; app preferences; and active workout or rest-timer information. Spot uses this information to provide workout planning, history, graphs, statistics, progress and preferences inside the app.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Spot uses Apple's local notification system to schedule rest-timer alerts on your device. These notifications are generated on the device and are not sent through a developer-operated notification server. Depending on the workout, an alert may include an exercise name or set information and may therefore be visible on your lock screen. You can disable notifications in Spot or in iOS Settings.

Backups and restoration

Spot creates a backup only when you choose to export one. A backup is a readable JSON file that may contain personal fitness information, including programmes, workout records, bodyweight measurements, goals, comments and preferences. On iOS, Spot temporarily prepares the file and opens the system share sheet so that you can choose where to save or share it. The file leaves Spot only through the destination you select, and that destination's privacy practices then apply. Store exported backups securely and share them only with services or people you trust.

Restoring a backup imports the selected file and replaces current Spot data after confirmation. Spot may keep a local pre-restore safety copy on the device. Spot cannot access or recover exported backup files for you.

Collection, transmission and sharing

Spot does not require an account and does not automatically transmit your fitness records to the developer or to a Spot server. The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking, and the developer does not sell personal information. Spot uses Apple and Capacitor device components for functions such as local notifications, file selection and sharing; using those components does not send your fitness records to the developer.

Contacting support

If you email Spot Support, the developer receives the email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to include. That information is used to respond to your enquiry. Your email provider and the support email provider also process the message under their own privacy terms. Avoid including sensitive information that is not needed to resolve your request.

Retention, deletion and security

App data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, use Clear All Data, or remove the app, subject to any device or iCloud backup restoration controlled by Apple. Clear All Data removes Spot-managed local app data, including any local pre-restore safety copy. It does not delete backup files that you exported to Files or another destination; you must delete those separately. Because Spot does not receive your locally stored fitness data, the developer cannot retrieve or delete it remotely. Spot relies on iOS and device security, so you should protect your device and exported backups.

Children and policy updates

Spot is not designed to collect children's personal information and does not knowingly transmit it to the developer. This policy may change if Spot's features or data practices change. The latest version and last-updated date will be published on this page.
